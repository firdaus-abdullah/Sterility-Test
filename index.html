02Purpose & Scope
Clause 1.0 and 2.0 of SOP QCSC.048.20.
1.0 PURPOSE

This procedure applies to the sterility testing of substances, preparations, or articles required to be sterile according to the Pharmacopeia.

KEY CONCEPT · USP <71> Sterility assurance cannot be measured by a sterility test — the test only demonstrates the absence of viable micro-organisms in the sample examined under the stated conditions.

2.0 SCOPE — applies to

1
Finished product & stability studies of sterile liquid parenteral preparation
2
Finished product & stability studies of sterile powder filling preparation
3
Finished product & stability studies of sterile ophthalmic and non-injectable preparation
4
Finished product & stability studies of sterile prefilled syringes
5
Sterile grade raw materials
6
Formulated Drug Product Solution (FDS)
7
Sterile grade packaging materials

05Test Sample Preparation
Clause 5.4 — unless otherwise specified in the individual test method in the product protocol of analysis (POA).
30UNITS MIN.

Sterile liquid parenteral & non-injectable preparation

Aseptically pool the entire content of at least 30 units of test sample in a pre-sterilized container.

30UNITS · 1,000 mL

Sterile powder filling preparation

Aseptically pool the entire content of at least 30 units of test sample in 1,000 mL of validated sterile rinse solution as stated in the individual rinse solution.

10UNITS MIN.

Sterile ophthalmic preparation

Aseptically pool the entire content of at least 10 units of test sample in a pre-sterilized container.

60UNITS MIN.

Sterile prefilled syringes

Aseptically pool the entire content of at least 60 units in a pre-sterilized container by directly expelling the content through the sterile needle.

100mg / mL

Sterile grade raw materials

Aseptically dilute the test sample in an appropriate volume of validated sterile rinse solution to make up a final concentration of 100 mg/mL.

Tip: Always verify the individual POA before pooling — the sample size and rinse solution may be product-specific.

07Direct Inoculation & Biological Indicator
Clauses 5.6 and 5.7 — specific to sterile-grade packaging materials.
🧴

5.6 Direct Inoculation

Sterile-grade packaging material
  • Aseptically transfer 10 units of test sample directly into 400 mL sterile FTM and another 10 units into 400 mL sterile TSB.
  • Make sure all test sample is completely immersed.
  • Negative control: use the same batch of uninoculated sterile test medium.
  • Incubate FTM at 30–35 °C for 14 days — aerobic & anaerobic bacteria.
  • Incubate TSB at 20–25 °C for 14 days — aerobic bacteria & fungi.
☢

5.7 Gamma-Irradiated Biological Indicator

Spore strips at outer primary packaging
  • Aseptically withdraw the sterilized spore strips using pre-sterilized forceps.
  • Direct inoculate the spore strips into sterile TSB.
  • Positive control: inoculate non-sterilized spore strips of a similar lot into sterile TSB.
  • Negative control: same batch of uninoculated sterile test medium.
  • Incubate at 30–35 °C for 7 days.
  • Record all observations in F.QC.062 — Biological Indicator Testing (Raw Data).
Interpretation: No growth on sterilized strips → sterilization was sufficient. No growth in the positive control → test invalid. Absence of growth in the negative control → media sterile prior to testing.
08Observation & Interpretation
Clause 5.8 — daily examination throughout the incubation period.
Day 0
Inoculation into FTM & TSB, plus negative control
Day 0 → 14
Examine daily for cloudiness, turbidity or clumps — record in F.QC.305
Day 18
For naturally thick / cloudy products: transfer ≥1 mL to fresh media at Day 14 and incubate 4 more days — record in F.QC.306
COMPLIES

No evidence of microbial growth

The product to be examined complies with the test for sterility.

INVALID

Growth in the negative control

The test may be considered invalid — investigate the media and aseptic technique.

NON-COMPLIANT

Growth in the sample

The product does not comply. Abort further incubation and investigate per SOP GQC.GLP.001 (OOS / OOT / atypical results).

Data integrity: A second analyst or reviewer examines / cross-checks all inoculated media at the end of incubation — or before incubation is aborted — prior to disposal or decontamination.
09Environmental & Personnel Monitoring
Clauses 5.9 and 5.10 — conducted during and at the end of sterility testing.
🌬

5.9 Environmental Monitoring

Dynamic — during the sterility test
  • Passive air sampling using TSA and SDAC plates.
  • Plates placed in the laminar airflow cabinet and associated background — K1 Sterility Test Room, K3 1L14 and K3 1L15 (MP1, MP2, MP5–MP8) per SOP QCSC.006.
  • Aseptically remove the lid and place face-down; record exact start time.
  • Maximum exposure per plate: 4 hours — then replace the lid and record end time.
  • Incubate TSA at 30–35 °C / 5 days; SDAC at 20–25 °C / 5 days, both inverted.
  • Count aerobic bacteria and fungi; record in Novatek EM Software.
  • Limits per SOP QCSC.006; deviations per SOP GQC.GLP.001.
🖐

5.10 Personnel Monitoring

Fingerprint sampling — end of testing
  • Performed at the end of the sterility testing by the fingerprint sampling method.
  • Inside the laminar airflow cabinet, aseptically remove the lid and place it face-down.
  • Press all 10 fingertips of both gloved hands simultaneously onto separate TSA plates with uniform, light pressure for 3–5 seconds; repeat with SDAC.
  • Close the plates and disinfect gloves with pre-sterilized 70% IPA.
  • Mark plates with analyst name, date and time of sample.
  • Incubate TSA at 30–35 °C / 5 days; SDAC at 20–25 °C / 5 days, both inverted.
  • Count and record in the Novatek EM Software; deviations per SOP GQC.GLP.001.

7.0 Appendix

Rinse solutions
  • Appendix 1 — Preparation of 0.1% w/v Peptone: dissolve 1 g peptic digest of animal tissue in deionized/purified water to 1,000 mL; adjust pH 7.1 ± 0.2; dispense; sterilize at 121 °C for 15 minutes; record in F.QC.162.
  • Appendix 2 — Preparation of 0.1% w/v Peptone with 1% Tween 80: dissolve 1 g peptic digest of animal tissue to 1,000 mL and add 10 g Polysorbate 80; adjust pH 7.1 ± 0.2; dispense; sterilize at 121 °C for 15 minutes; record in F.QC.161.
